# Editorial imagery — November 2026

Six original conceptual illustrations generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for Victor Nogueira's portfolio. These are project cover illustrations, not screenshots or evidence of production deployments. Interface titles, project details and controls remain native HTML. Existing interactive demos are unchanged.

## Art direction and prompt set

Hero: a premium editorial industrial-design illustration of information becoming a reliable decision. Three staggered milky acrylic and matte charcoal plates on satin aluminium rails. Small scattered charcoal data tiles enter one end; restrained translucent acid-lime tracks pass through ordered layers and resolve into a sharp lime indicator. Elevated three-quarter view, credible fabricated desktop instrument, realistic shadows and materials. Paper #f4f6f1, ink #101816, lime #d9f85c. Central subject, generous crop-safe margins. No letters, logos, numbers, fake metrics, UI or science-fiction scenery.

Shared cover prompt: Create one finished raster editorial portfolio project cover, landscape 16:9, no text, no lettering, no numerals, no logos, no charts or software interface. A carefully fabricated miniature industrial sculpture, photographed from a 35 degree elevated three-quarter angle, subtle real material grain, tactile rounded milled edges, restrained fine detail, believable studio shadows, premium design-magazine art direction. Main object occupies central 75%, pale warm paper background #f4f6f1, matte charcoal #101816 and satin aluminium/acrylic. Avoid generic AI blobs, floating balls, purple gradients and busy background. It is a conceptual illustration, not a screenshot.

- SignalPath: Growth and acquisition paths: an elegant physical branching channel instrument, three translucent pale-blue branching tracks funnel tiny charcoal rectangular tiles through precise aligned gates and converge into one clear destination tray. Small acid lime highlight at final junction. Minimal information architecture expressed physically. Blue accent #a8cbff.
- AisleFlow: Retail inventory planning: architectural miniature of three low modular shelving rows with neatly grouped matte charcoal small cartons, translucent amber shelf dividers and selected warm amber inventory units. A compact sliding reorder rail connects shelves. Crisp top-down legibility, sophisticated minimal composition. Amber accent #f7b96e.
- Clearline: Customer service evidence organization: three staggered frosted acrylic document-like panels with tiny engraved abstract line patterns that cannot be read as text; several charcoal rectangular evidence chips sorted into three deliberate mint-green trays underneath, connected by slim metal rails. Tactile, sparse, premium. Mint accent #aee7c7.
- PortSignal: Maritime logistics decision operations: detailed but elegantly minimal architectural port miniature: two container vessels at separate berths, stacked small containers, three silver quay cranes, narrow cyan translucent route strips through dark navy acrylic ocean base. A single small amber container marks priority. Navy #071e32 and cyan accent. No actual maps, flags, text or illuminated science fiction.
- Metric Lab: Reconciling reports: two precisely aligned matte white ledger-like plates side by side on a charcoal rail base. Each plate has ordered rows of tiny graphite rectangular tiles; a few mismatched tiles are lifted slightly and highlighted in translucent acid-lime acrylic. A slim transparent bridge physically matches the corresponding rows between plates. Clean lime #d9f85c accent, clear visual metaphor of comparing two records.

## Web exports

The hero is exported at 800 and 1600 pixels wide; covers at 600 and 1200 pixels. WebP export changes resolution and encoding only. Both languages share these files through responsive `srcset`; below-the-fold covers load lazily. Original generated PNGs are archived outside the published repository on drive D.
